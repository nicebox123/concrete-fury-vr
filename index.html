Controls

Meta Quest

  • Left stick move (click: sprint)
  • Right stick snap/smooth turn · up: jump · down: crouch
  • Grip grab / climb · point + grip pulls items to you
  • Trigger fire · menu select
  • A / X on gun hand: drop mag / open cylinder
  • B / Y on gun hand: rack slide / close cylinder
  • Grip at belt with a gun in the other hand: take ammo
  • Rack: grab the slide or pump and pull back
  • Holsters: let go near your hip or over your shoulder
  • Y (empty hand) / hold Y pause menu · X spawn menu (sandbox)

Desktop

  • WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · C crouch
  • Mouse look · LMB fire / swing / punch
  • E grab / drop (pulls items you look at)
  • RMB throw held item · F kick
  • R reload · Q swap holstered weapon
  • Tab spawn menu (sandbox) · Esc pause
